Lesson 1

🎨 Adjectives = the Paint Brush

🖌️
Adjectives PAINT a noun. They tell us WHAT KIND.
Big or small? What colour? What feeling? What taste?
tall giraffe · red car · happy girl
🦒
tall giraffe
size
🚗
red car
colour
😊
happy girl
feeling
🛏️
soft pillow

🥭

Remember: an adjective goes before the noun (a red car) or after is / feels / seems (the car is red).
It never adds "s": two big dogs ✅ (not "bigs").

Stairs of comparing: 🐭 small → 🐶 smaller → 🐘 smallest (tall → taller → tallest)

👉
Not an adjective job: "Which one?", "Whose?" and "How many?" belong to determiners. See Lesson 2!

Lesson 2

👉 Determiners = P · O · C

👆🎁🔢
Determiners come before a noun. Sort them with P-O-C: Point, Own, Count.
They come first, before any adjective.

👆 P = Point "Which one?"

thisthatthesethosethewhichwhat

this book · those shoes · the sun · which road? · what colour?

Grown-up names: demonstratives, "the", question words.

🎁 O = Own "Whose one?"

myyourhisheritsourtheirwhose

my bag · her coat · our house · whose bag?

Grown-up names: possessives, "whose".

🔢 C = Count "How many? How much?"

Numbersone, two, three · first, second, third
Onea, an (they mean "one")
How many? 🍎🍎many, few, several, both, a couple of
How much? 💧much, little, a little
Both kindssome, any, no, all, more, most, enough
One at a timeeach, every, either, neither

Grown-up names: numbers, quantifiers, distributives, "a / an".

🧠 Remember

  • a / an are in Count because they mean "one". the is in Point because it points to a special noun.
  • 👆 Point and 🎁 Own never go together: the my book ❌
  • 🔢 Count can tag along after them: my two cats ✅ · these three books ✅ · all the boys ✅

Lesson 3

🦸 Helping Verbs = the Sidekicks

🦸‍♂️🤝🏃
A helping verb is the SIDEKICK of the main verb.
The hero (main verb) does the action. The sidekick adds time or power.
She is reading · I can swim

Team 1: The Primary Trio 👨‍👩‍👧 (be · have · do)

🏃
BE
am is are was were
"is running" (happening now)
✅
HAVE
have has had
"have finished" (done!)
❓
DO
do does did
"Do you like it?" / "I did not go"

They have special powers, never change shape, and the next verb stays in its simple form.

Lesson 4

❓ Questions = the Flip

🔄
To ask a question, FLIP the sidekick to the front!
Statement: She is coming. → Question: Is she coming?

1️⃣ Yes / No question — the Sandwich 🥪

Helper + Who + Action ?
Can you swim?

No helper in the sentence? Call a free one: do / does / did 📞
You like tea. → Do you like tea?

2️⃣ Wh- question — the Detective 🕵️

Where do you live?
Who 🧑 person
What 📦 thing
Where 📍 place
When ⏰ time
Why 🤔 reason
How 🛠️ way

⚠️ Special case: if the question word IS the doer, no flip needed: Who called you? ✅

3️⃣ Question tag — the Opposite Twin 👯

Positive sentence → negative tag. Negative sentence → positive tag.
You are coming, aren't you? · She doesn't smoke, does she?
