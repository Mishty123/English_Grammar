Some and Any

Which word to pick depends on two things: the kind of sentence (positive, negative or question) and the kind of noun.

Colours someanya / an or no word

Two kinds of noun

Countable

You can count them. They have a singular and a plural form.

dog, dogs  ·  book, books

Uncountable

You cannot count them. They have one form only and take a singular verb.

water  ·  time  ·  information (The information was helpful.)
