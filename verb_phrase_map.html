The big map

A verb phrase has 1, 2, 3 or 4 roles, counted from the left. Role 1 is finite, and the last role is always the lexical verb.

Finite verb → tense Whole verb phrase → aspect and voice Lexical verb → clause pattern
Rule 1

Role 1 is finite

Role 1 is always the finite verb. It shows tense and agrees with the subject. Every later role is non-finite. Finite or not →

Rule 2

The whole phrase builds aspect & voice

The helper in Role 1 plus the forms after it (be + V4, have + V3, be + V3) decide continuous, perfect or passive. Role 1 alone tells only the tense. Aspect & voice →

Rule 3

The last role is the lexical verb

The last role, the lexical verb, decides what comes next: None, an Object, a Complement, or an Adverbial when one is required. Clause patterns →
